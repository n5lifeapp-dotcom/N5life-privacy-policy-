N5 Life Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

N5 Life ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the N5 Life mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use N5 Life, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Profile photo, if provided
  • Location information, when permission is granted
  • Service and booking information
  • Address provided for service requests
  • Information you voluntarily provide while using the app

2. Google Sign-In

N5 Life may provide Google Sign-In as an authentication option. When you use Google Sign-In, we may receive basic account information such as your name, email address, and profile photo according to the permissions provided by Google.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide home-service booking functionality
  • Connect customers with service professionals
  • Process and manage service requests
  • Communicate with you regarding bookings
  • Improve the N5 Life application
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse

4. Location Information

N5 Life may request access to your device location to provide location-related features such as identifying your service area and helping connect you with nearby professionals.

You can control location permissions through your device settings.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share relevant information with service professionals when necessary to fulfill a service request.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

7. Data Storage

N5 Life may use third-party services such as Firebase to securely store and manage application data and authentication information.

8. Children's Privacy

N5 Life is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Your Choices

You may manage certain permissions, including location permissions, through your device settings. You may also contact us regarding questions about your personal information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
